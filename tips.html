Tips for Managing Screen Time

Remember:

Self care is important so take breaks from screens regularly and maintain a healthy balance.

Tip 1

Set a daily screen-time goal.

Tip 2

Reduce the limit by 15–30 minutes each week.

Tip 3

Turn off unnecessary notifications.

Tip 4

Keep your phone away while studying or sleeping.

Tip 5

Use app timers for distracting apps.

Tip 6

Replace screen time with hobbies, exercise, reading, or time with friends.

Tip 7

Set a specific “no-phone” period each day.

Tip 8

Track your progress and reward yourself for meeting your goal.

Tip 9

If the limit is set by a parent/guardian, talk with them about changing it together.
